# No product combines an open plan format with mixed-team capacity, LLM authoring and tracker round-trip

Tags: competition

## Rationale

As of November 2026, "planning as code" has no canonical standard. Surveyed across scheduling tools, agent frameworks and spec-driven development kits, no product combines all four elements of this project's format: an open YAML source of truth, a task DAG with roles and per-executor capacity for mixed human/agent teams, authoring via LLM structured interview, and status round-trip with Jira/Linear. Nearest misses are recorded as separate facts; each lacks at least two of the four elements.

Stale when any competitor ships the combination — recheck the two open tracker-sync RFCs (see [interview-to-ticket-tools-leave-tracker-sync-open](interview-to-ticket-tools-leave-tracker-sync-open.md)) before relying on this in positioning.

## Evidence

- Web research session of 2026-11-02 (ZCode, three grilling rounds), recorded in the handoff of 2026-11-03: surveyed TaskJuggler, CrewAI, GitHub Spec Kit, Kiro, OpenSpec, Height and the planning-as-code landscape.
